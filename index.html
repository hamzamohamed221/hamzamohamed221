Questions

How long does a project take?

Social posts and posters usually take 1 to 3 days, a logo 3 to 5 days, a landing page UI 5 to 7 days and a full brand identity 7 to 10 days. If you have a deadline, tell me and I’ll tell you honestly if it’s possible.

How many revisions do I get?

Every package includes revisions, listed on each card. If you need more rounds, we can agree on them before we start.

What files will I receive?

Final files in the formats you need: PNG or JPG for social media, PDF for print, and editable source files (AI, PSD or Figma) for brand and UI projects.

How does payment work?

I start after a deposit of 50%, and send the final files when the balance is paid. We agree on the scope and price in writing first, so there are no surprises.

Can you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work remotely with clients anywhere. The easiest way to start is a WhatsApp message with a short description of what you need.
